# Record Minors as Their Own Visits Linked to an Acompañante or a Residente que recibe

Ley 1581 (art. 7) and Decreto 1074 (art. 2.2.2.25.2.9) let the copropiedad process a minor's data only with the authorization of the minor's legal representative, which may be given by unequivocal conduct, and the preliminary legal review of the pilot found that a "Menor de edad" switch with an optional document left no record of who authorized. Every Visitante menor de edad therefore gets their own Visita, with its own Ingreso and Salida, that always links to whoever answers for the minor: the Visita of an adult Acompañante registered in the same Ingreso, or a Residente que recibe from the destination Apartamento (chosen by the Portero after an in-person or intercom confirmation, or the Residente who created the minor's Autorización). A minor arriving without an Acompañante cannot enter an Apartamento with no active Residente.

## Considered Options

- **A list of accompanying minors inside the adult's Visita.** Simpler, but it cannot represent a minor who stays after the adult leaves (a child dropped off at a party) or one who arrives alone, and the Portero would lose track of which children are still inside.

## Consequences

- A minor's Visita inherits its origin (Pase or manual) from the Acompañante's Visita, so an adult with a Pase who brings children does not inflate the manual count in the Turno metrics.
- The Acompañante's Salida also records the Salida of their minors still inside; annulling the Acompañante's Visita annuls those minors' open Visitas with the same reason.
- The document is optional and recorded only from age 14 (any type except CC); plates are never recorded. This departs from the preliminary review, which said "never a document", on the grounds that Ley 1581 art. 12 b only makes answering optional. A real lawyer must confirm it before the pilot.
- Anonymization erases the minor's name and document but keeps the link, which no longer identifies the minor on its own.
